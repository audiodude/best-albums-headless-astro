Not much of a sleeper pick here, but this album definitely rocks. See my previous comments about Blur's "Parklife" about how I should be writing about British invasions. "Champagne Supernova" is an epic closer that wraps things up nicely and lets you drift away. Although this album is a contender for having won the loudness wars of the 90s and 2000s, the song construction is top notch even if the mixes are a bit harsh. Overall an amazing album.
