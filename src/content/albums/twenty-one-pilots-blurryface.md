Everyone knows this album because everyone knows "Stressed Out", and that's a good song. But you know that feeling when people tell you "Oh that hit single isn't even representative of that band at all"? That's not true for "Stressed Out" and Twenty One Pilots. What is true is that the single barely represents the diversity on this album. This album is a true inspiration for me because it's a group of talented creative people making whatever music they want, from uk garage, to songs that sound like Ben Folds, songs with ukulele, just everything. I wouldn't say they *seamlessly blend* these genres really, but the breadth and quality on this album makes it one of the best in the universe.
