Keasbey Nights explodes out of your stereo from the first playing. It is frenetically upbeat music, even as the lyrics explore themes of isolation and desperation. This album is one of the best in the universe, as it captures the best parts of third wave ska with unrelenting punk drumming and guitars and catchy horn lines throughout.
