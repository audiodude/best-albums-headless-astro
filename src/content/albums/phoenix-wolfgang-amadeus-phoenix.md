I believe the genre of this amazing album is sometimes called "indie pop". It features catchy, upbeat songs with heavy use of synthesizers and drum machines, mixed and mastered to perfect pop precision. There have been a lot of artists clearly influenced by late 2000s records like this one, but this collection will always have a special place in my heart. Oh and it won a Grammy.
