Though at times the lyrics are a bit too wink-wink and tongue in cheek, the musical content simply pumps through your speakers and never lets go. The smash hits "Dance, Dance" and "Sugar, We're Going Down" feature alongside 11 other songs that actually work seamlessly as a group. Anxiety and angst never seemed so sexy as it tromps through a sound that is distinctive and familiar, yet still unique. Definitely one of the best albums in the universe.
