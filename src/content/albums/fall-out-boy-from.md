This might be a no-brainer pick for Fall Out Boy, but we never said this site was about being clever. Though at times the lyrics are a bit too wink-wink and tongue in cheek, the musical content simply pumps through your speakers and never lets go. The smash hits "Dance, Dance" and "Sugar, We're Going Down" feature alongside 11 other songs that have distinctive sound and work seamlessly as a group. Anxiety and angst never seemed so sexy as it tromps through a sound that is distinctive and familiar, yet still unique. Definitely one of the best albums in the universe.
