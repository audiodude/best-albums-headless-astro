Okay yes, this is the album with "Stacy's Mom". But there's so much more here. Through smart arrangements and great production values, what really shines on Welcome Interstate Managers is the tight songwriting. It seems like every song, in the variety of subtle genres, has motion, is going somewhere, and there's very little filler or indulgence. Great album, great rock songs, and the conceits of the songs are delightful not corny.
