The dance-y synthpop, punk and hip-hop influenced sound of Matt and Kim's third studio album is certainly infectious. The duo benefits greatly from recording in a professional studio, pumping out highly polished anthemic synth jams. It's impossible to listen to this album and not move, though sometimes it might be more of a jerky twitch than actual dancing.
