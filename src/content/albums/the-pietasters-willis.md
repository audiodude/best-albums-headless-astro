On *Willis*, The Pietasters go hard into the soul and "rocksteady" side of things. The horns are swinging, not punching you in the face, and the vocals pack a ton of swagger. There's even a Toots cover, because of course there is. The production works, but is not so polished that it covers up the feeling of nodding along in a bar to good vibes while occassionally getting up to skank. One of the best third wave ska albums, and also one of the best in the universe.
