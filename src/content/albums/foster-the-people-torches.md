Do you remember "Pumped Up Kicks"? It was everywhere in 2010/2011. But *Torches* has much more substance than just that single. If I were ever to use the term "indie pop", it would be here. It has more than enough bouncy synths and falsetto vocal lines to keep me coming back for more. Great balance of danceable tracks with more indie/rock instrumentation and arrangements. This albums is fun, clever and deserves to be called one of the best in the universe.
